01 Bio

Short bio

We are a four-piece band playing original songs that mix punk & funk energy, pop melodies, and alternative and indie guitar sounds. Two guitars, bass and drums, all original music. We wanna get loud and make you feel.

Full bio

Midnight Yoga has been around since the end of summer 2024. We write our own music, inspired by our own brain and genres such as psychedelic, indie, and actually all other types of rock, funk, punk but also country, folk and pop. This makes our music unique and interesting but mainly catchy and fun. We are a group of three men and one woman, who came together when we were looking for a band to express ourselves in. With two guitars, a bass, drums and up to three voices we create a wall of sound to carry you away. We have performed 7 times with only our own music so far and have released our first single and are working on doing the same for our first EP.
